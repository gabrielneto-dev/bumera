# Goals

## Goals
- Deliver a simple, cheap, easy-to-implement follow-up system for dental clinics (modules 1–3).
- Run pilots with clinics to validate modules 1–3.
- Sell at a low entry price (indicative ~R$400 setup + ~R$200/month), then evolve plans and features.
- Later: AI WhatsApp assistant (module 4) for 3 paying clients.
- Long term: grow into an "ecosystem".

## Non-goals (for now)
- AI in the initial phase (only module 4, later).
- A free diagnosis/report offering was discussed and explicitly dropped.
