# Overview

**Bumera** (dir name; the user's dictated "Boomera" is assumed to be the same thing) is intended to grow into an ecosystem for service businesses. Its first concrete shape is a **simple, low-cost system for dental clinics** that automates patient follow-up and communication, mainly over WhatsApp.

## Modules (initial scope)
1. **Reativação** — contact patients who have not appeared for ~6 months (e.g. for a check-up).
2. **Follow-up de orçamento** — follow up patients who received a treatment plan/quote and did not close.
3. **Confirmação e lembrete** — confirm appointments and send reminders to reduce no-shows.
4. **Atendimento de IA no WhatsApp** — answer, qualify and schedule. Built last; only for 3 paying clients.

Plus a simple scheduling interface (client name, CPF, a few other fields).

## Business shape
Pilots first, then paid clients. Indicative price: ~R$400 setup + ~R$200/month (not final). Early phase deliberately avoids AI to keep it cheap.
