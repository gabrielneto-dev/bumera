# Constraints

- Must be simple, easy to implement and cheap to operate (user-stated).
- Low price point implies low per-client infra/message cost; no AI in the initial phase.
- Module 4 (AI WhatsApp) is limited to 3 paying clients and comes last.
- Target domain: dental clinics (Brazil — CPF, WhatsApp, R$).
- Patient data (CPF + health context) is sensitive; see `CTX-RSK-20261014-lgpd-patient-data`.
