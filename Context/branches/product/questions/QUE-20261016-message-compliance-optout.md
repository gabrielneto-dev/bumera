## Question
Como tratar consentimento e opt-out das mensagens aos pacientes?

## Why this matters
Reactivation/follow-up messages are proactive; need consent and a way to stop messages.

## What depends on the answer
Flow rules, ban-risk mitigation (`CTX-RSK-20261014-whatsapp-unofficial-api`).

## Known options
Opt-in at registration, "SAIR" keyword, per-patient do-not-contact flag, message templates and rate limits.
