# Decision

## Context
Early product, low price point, solo builder, goal of simple/cheap/easy to implement.

## Decision
Build reativação, follow-up de orçamento and confirmação/lembrete first; build the AI WhatsApp assistant last, offered to only 3 paying clients.

## Rationale
(User-stated) keep the initial system simple and monetarily cheap; avoid AI initially; the low price depends on that.

## Consequences
Pilots target modules 1–3. Module 4 timing depends on those validating.

## Risks
Order among modules 1–3 not specified (see pilot question).
