# Decision
Three roles per clinic: clinic owner/administrator (creates the clinic account, configures messages and triggers), secretary (registers patients, manages the calendar, confirms registrations originating from WhatsApp) and dentist (own agenda, registers finished procedures that fire triggers). Proposed by the agent and approved by the user, who had initially named only "the dentist and the clinic".
